About FoodGuard

What is Food Image Fraud Detection?

With the rise of AI-generated images and advanced image editing tools, it has become increasingly difficult to trust food images online. This can have serious implications for food delivery platforms, restaurant reviews, and food safety documentation.

The Food Image Fraud Detector uses a multi-stage forensic analysis pipeline to determine whether a food image is genuine (photographed in the real world) or fake (AI-generated or digitally manipulated).

How It Works

Our system processes each image through 6 forensic stages:

1
Metadata Analysis

Examines EXIF data, file properties, and header information for anomalies that indicate manipulation.

2
Preprocessing

Standardizes images through resizing, normalization, and color space conversions for consistent analysis.

3
Spatial Feature Analysis

Detects artifacts in pixel patterns, edge irregularities, and color distribution anomalies (18 features).

4
Frequency Analysis

Applies Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to detect unusual frequency patterns typical of AI-generated images (10 features).

5
Region-Based Analysis

Divides the image into a 4x4 grid and detects statistically anomalous patches (8 features).

6
ML Classification

A Random Forest classifier combines all 40 extracted features to produce a final fraud probability score and risk assessment.

Dataset

The model was trained on a labeled dataset of real food photographs and AI-generated/fake food images. Real images are captured from actual food items, while fake images are synthetically generated to mimic food photography.
